What it does

A coach for the hard hour.

Checks in before your hard hour.

Steady learns when a fast gets hard for you. The coach checks in just before that hour.

Mood check-ins.

Log how you feel in two taps. Steady asks again later, so you can see if the feeling passed.

Your own record.

See the fasts you finished and how your check-ins changed. It is your record, shown back to you.

Private by design.

No account. Your data stays on your phone. Fasting windows run from 12 to 20 hours.
