What it does

Built for the hour you usually quit.

Checks in before your hard hour.

Tell Steady which hour usually decides your fast. It checks in half an hour before it.

Mood check-ins.

Log how you feel in two taps. Get three minutes of slow breathing or a small puzzle. About two hours later, Steady checks back in.

Your own record.

How many fasts you finished, and your longest. Both can only go up. There is no streak to lose.

Private by design.

No account, and nothing you enter leaves your phone. Fasting windows run from 12 to 20 hours.
