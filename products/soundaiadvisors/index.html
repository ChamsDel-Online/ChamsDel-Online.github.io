What it is

AI that actually makes it to production.

Most AI projects in regulated companies stall in the same place. The strategy deck looks great. The pilot demos well. Then legal, security, or compliance reviews it, and it never ships. Sound AI Advisors exists to close that gap.

Who it's for.

Fintech and B2B SaaS companies in the $20M-$200M range, operating under SOC 2, PCI, HIPAA, or GDPR. CTOs, CISOs, heads of engineering, and founders who need AI in production but cannot trade compliance for speed.

Why it exists.

Twenty years inside regulated companies, including PayPal, taught me how AI projects die. They die at the audit. They die when the runbook does not exist. They die when no one owns the model in production. This practice ships workflows that survive all three.

How it works

Three phases. No theater.

01

Discovery.

A 30-minute call to identify the highest-leverage workflow inside your stack. Not a fishing trip. We leave with a target workflow, a rough scope, and a clear yes or no on whether to proceed.

02

Build.

4-8 weeks embedded with your team. We design, build, and deploy the workflow into production with audit-ready documentation alongside the code. Not a side experiment. Inside your environment.

03

Handoff.

Ownership transfers to your team with runbooks, monitoring, and the documentation an auditor expects. You leave with a working system your engineers can maintain, not a dependency on me.

Sample workflows

The shape of a 4-8 week engagement.

Specific. Bounded. Audit-ready on the way out. These are illustrative starting points; real engagements get scoped to your stack and your risk profile.

Fintech

Vendor security questionnaire automation.

Replace the manual slog of answering SIG, CAIQ, and custom security questionnaires with an AI workflow grounded in your real controls documentation. Auditable. Reviewable. Faster sales cycles.

B2B SaaS

Customer security response drafting.

Inbound security and compliance questions from prospects, drafted in your voice, sourced from your evidence. Your security team reviews and sends. Time on prospect questionnaires drops by an order of magnitude.

B2B SaaS

Tier-1 customer support with audit logs.

Front-line support backed by your help center and product docs, with every response logged for review. Reduce handle time on the easy 70%. Keep the audit trail your compliance team needs.

Fintech

KYC/KYB document processing.

Structured extraction from incorporation documents, beneficial ownership filings, and identity documents, with confidence scores and human-in-the-loop review for everything below threshold.
