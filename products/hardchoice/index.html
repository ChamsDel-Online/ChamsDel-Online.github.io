HardChoice

A structured decision tool for couples navigating high-stakes choices. Less friction, clearer thinking. Built for decisions around career, family, money, and identity, where tradeoffs are hard to hold in your head.

Visit HardChoice.app Read the launch update All products
What it does

Structure, not oversimplification.

Who it is for.

Couples navigating decisions around career, family, money, or identity where tradeoffs are hard to hold in your head.

Why this approach works.

Decision tools fail when they oversimplify. HardChoice focuses on structure that helps couples surface tradeoffs without flattening the emotional reality of the decision.
