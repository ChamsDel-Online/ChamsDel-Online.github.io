CampfireX

CampfireX helps creators turn cold audiences into warm communities on X by surfacing the right conversations to host and the right people to gather around them. Less broadcasting, more belonging.

Visit CampfireX Read the product story All products
What it does

Gather the right people around the right conversation.

Who it is for.

Creators, founders, and operators who want their X presence to feel like a community rather than a feed of one-off posts, and who care more about depth of connection than raw follower count.

Why it exists.

X rewards constant posting, but real community comes from recurring conversations with the same people. CampfireX makes it easier to spot those people, host conversations they actually return to, and turn engagement into something durable.
