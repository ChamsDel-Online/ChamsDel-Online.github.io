Privacy policy

KeptCue · updated 2026-11-09

KeptCue keeps your data on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in, no tracking and no advertising, and the jobs you track are never sent anywhere. This page says exactly what is stored and how to delete all of it.

What KeptCue stores, and where

Everything KeptCue knows lives in a local database on your phone. We never upload, sync or back it up. If you delete the app, it is gone.

  • The jobs you add: their names, how often they repeat, which kind they are (rare or everyday), an optional "do it by" time and an optional "do it after" job.
  • The dates you marked each job done, including dates you entered for the past.
  • Your reminder choices and reminder times.
  • Your answers in the first-run setup, which are used to suggest jobs and a reminder time.
  • Your home details: whether you own or rent, roughly when you moved in, and what the home has (for example a furnace, a fireplace or a septic system). These pick the jobs in your first-year plan.
  • Which first-year plan jobs you have ticked off.

Purchases

Payment is handled by Apple. KeptCue never sees your card, your Apple Account or your name. Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which holds an anonymous identifier for this install and the state of your subscription. Two setup answers are also attached to that anonymous record: whether you own or rent your home, and whether it is a new build. We use them only to understand, in total, who KeptCue is useful for. That identifier, your subscription state and those two answers are the only things about you that exist outside your phone. Your jobs, dates, move-in date and other home details are never sent to RevenueCat.

Notifications

All reminders are scheduled on your phone. There is no push server, and no notification token is created or sent anywhere. By default the lock screen shows a generic reminder; job names appear there only if you switch that on in Settings.

What KeptCue does not do

  • No analytics SDK, crash reporting or usage tracking. Beyond your subscription, the two answers described under Purchases are all we see.
  • No advertising, and no data shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No account, and no way for us to look up an individual user.

Your control

Settings has "save a backup", which hands you everything KeptCue holds as a single file, "save as a spreadsheet", and "delete all data", which erases all of it and returns the app to its first screen. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. Because nothing is stored on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, and nothing we could recover if you asked.

Backups and moving to a new phone

KeptCue never uploads your data, but your phone might. Apple includes the app's storage in your iCloud device backup, like any other app, so setting up a new phone from that backup can bring your jobs with it. That copy is between you and Apple, under Apple's privacy terms; we have no access to it.

Children

KeptCue is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. We will not quietly widen what leaves your phone.
