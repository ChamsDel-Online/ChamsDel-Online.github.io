NicheSlap

A free X account analyzer for founders who keep changing their niche. NicheSlap reads your public posts and profile, lays every niche, bio and offer you have tried on one timeline, and shows which one actually earned you views and replies.

Scan your X account See a demo report All products
What it does

See it. Find the one that was working. Lock it in.

See it.

Every niche, bio, pin, link and banner you have cycled through, on one timeline you can drag through, with a Whiplash score for how often you pivot.

Find the one that was working.

It compares what each niche earned you in views and replies, so you can see whether an earlier niche was doing better than the one you moved to.

Lock it in.

Pick one niche and commit for 30 days. NicheSlap checks your profile and posts every day and emails you the day you start drifting.

Pricing

The scan is free.

The Slap. Free.

Your Whiplash score, a timeline of every niche you have tried, the niche graveyard, your content playbook with one pillar complete, your best opening lines, and a share card for X. No password, no card.

Stay Slapped. $9.99 once, for 30 days.

Which niche was actually working, ranked with real numbers, the full playbook, and the 30-day lock with daily checks. One payment. It does not auto-renew.

How it works

Public data only.

Where the data comes from.

Your public posts and public profile. Nothing needs your X password or a login with X. Reading a year of posts takes a few minutes, and the report link is emailed to you.

What it can and cannot see.

X does not keep a public history of bios, so past niches are rebuilt from your posts. Your bio, pin, link and banner are recorded every day from your first scan onward. NicheSlap is not affiliated with X Corp.
