Legal entity after the first dollar

In the US you can take money tomorrow as a sole proprietor with a personal Stripe account. An LLC, EIN and business bank account before any revenue is how careful people feel productive without shipping. We form it the week after, or in parallel, never as a gate. The exception is an employer clause that makes the entity matter now.

Web or Chrome first, iPhone second

No review queue, no 30 percent cut, no developer fee, and the first dollar can land in week two. If it has to be an iPhone app, you enroll with Apple as an individual on day one of Setup, which takes days, we ship to TestFlight in the sprint, and the first dollar comes from a founding-member link while review runs.

Not in the US? You do not need a US company to get paid

Setting up a US LLC, a US tax number and a US bank account from abroad takes about two months and is a miserable first experience. Instead, you use a service that sells your product on your behalf, collects the money, handles sales tax, and pays you out to your local bank. Lemon Squeezy and Paddle do exactly this, and Stripe works directly in more than 45 countries. A US company comes later, if you ever need one.
