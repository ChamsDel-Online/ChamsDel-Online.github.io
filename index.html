Also: AI advisory

I help regulated companies roll out AI.

Get AI approved by security, then get it used. The day job is running exactly this inside a fintech under PCI and SOC 2, so the advice comes from doing it, not from a deck.

01

Govern

Count the AI already in use. Write the policy, the approved tool list and the data rules from what people actually do.

02

Enable

Put approved tools in the hands of one or two pilot teams. Training, a champion in each team, weekly office hours.

03

Measure

Track who uses it, what it saves and why people stop. Turn that into a report the board can read.

Who it's for: fintech, payments, healthcare-adjacent and insurance companies of about 50 to 500 people. Usually sponsored by the CEO or COO. It starts with a two-week AI Readiness Review.

The pattern

Start bad. Stay. Transform. Repeat.

Every real change in my life followed the same shape: uncomfortable at the start, boring in the middle, a different person at the end. Building in public at 43 is just the latest rep.

Speaking
Stage fright → Toastmaster

Public speaking used to terrify me. I joined Toastmasters and did the reps until the fear stopped running the room.

Discipline
White belt → 2x black belt

Two black belts, earned one class at a time. Mastery is mostly showing up long after it stops being exciting.

Money
$0 → $1M by 35

From zero to my first million by 35. Slow, patient, compounding decisions beat one big swing every time.

Identity
Introvert → Building in public at 43

Still an introvert. I just stopped letting that be an excuse to stay invisible. Now I build, ship and post in the open.
