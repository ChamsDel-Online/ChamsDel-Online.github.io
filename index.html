Origin Story

This is what it looks like when a stable life starts dreaming louder.

The story is not about escaping a career. It is about expanding a life. Years in tech taught me how to operate at scale. Fatherhood taught me why time matters. Indie making became the place where those two truths collided.

Chapter 01

Built a serious career. Refused to let that be the final form.

I spent more than two decades growing in technology, leading through complexity, reliability, and risk. The work mattered. But eventually another question started following me around: what would happen if I took all of that experience and pointed it at my own ideas?

Chapter 02

Fatherhood changed the economics of attention.

When you are a dad, every hour has a name on it. That pressure made me more intentional. Less fantasy. More shipping. I learned to build in the margins, where focus has to be earned.

Chapter 03

43 became a starting line.

Not because it was convenient. Because it was honest. I knew enough to stop romanticizing entrepreneurship and start practicing it with discipline.

Chapter 04

The goal is not to look busy. The goal is to make things that earn their place in someone’s life.

Every experiment on this page is part of that pursuit: useful tools, thoughtful interfaces, and proof that reinvention does not belong only to the young or unburdened.

Dual Life

Manifesto

The journey in four deliberate moves.

01. Consistency
I keep showing up even when the available time is small. Progress comes from repeated effort inside ordinary weeks, not from rare bursts of perfect focus.
02. Transparency
I share the work as it is, including the unfinished parts, the missed assumptions, and the lessons that only appear after shipping.
03. Creativity
I do not want to build lifeless tools. I want each product to carry originality, feeling, and a point of view that makes it worth remembering.
04. Longivity
This journey is built to last. I am not chasing a short spike of attention, but a sustainable practice of making meaningful work over many years.
