How to use it

  1. Build the policy, then read it as an employee would. Cut anything your company will not do.
  2. Fill in the brackets, then ask your security and compliance leads to review it.
  3. Share it with one pilot team first, and add what the pilot shows you were missing.
  4. Keep a written list of exceptions with an owner and an end date. Review it on the date in the policy.

Frequently asked questions

What should an AI acceptable use policy include?

The approved tools, the data that never goes into any AI tool, what needs approval first, who to ask, how to report a mistake, and who owns the policy and when it is reviewed. This tool builds a one-page policy with those parts.

Does this tool send what I enter anywhere?

No. The policy is built in your browser. What you type is not sent to a server. The site's analytics only counts that the tool was used, not what you entered.

Is the policy legal advice?

No. It is a starting point. Have your legal, security and compliance leads review it before you publish it to your team.

Why one page?

A long policy is written before anyone knows how the tools get used, and few people read it. Start with one page covering approved tools, data that never goes in, and who to ask. Then let the first pilot show what else the policy needs.
