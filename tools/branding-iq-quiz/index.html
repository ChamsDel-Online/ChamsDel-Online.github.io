The five pillars of branding IQ

A strong brand isn't one thing, it's five working together. Niche clarity is who you serve and what pain you solve. Positioning is why you and not someone else. Voice is how your writing sounds. Visual identity is whether your look is consistent and intentional. Content output is whether you can actually ship in your brand's voice, week after week, at a rate that compounds. Frequently asked questions

What does this branding quiz test?

Five fundamentals: niche clarity (who you serve and what pain you solve), positioning (why you and not someone else), voice (how your writing sounds), visual identity (whether your look is consistent and intentional), and content output (whether you ship in your brand's voice week after week).

How long does the branding quiz take?

Under three minutes. 15 multiple-choice questions, scored instantly in your browser. No signup, no data sent anywhere.

What does my branding IQ score mean?

Scores are out of 100 across five pillars. 80+ means your branding foundation is strong. 60, 79 means you have a solid base but one pillar is holding you back. 40, 59 means multiple pillars need work. Below 40 means it's time to rebuild from niche up.

What's the difference between a brand archetype quiz and a branding quiz?

A brand archetype quiz identifies the personality your brand embodies (Sage, Rebel, Hero, etc.). A branding quiz tests whether your overall brand system, niche, voice, identity, output, is strong. Archetype is one input; branding IQ measures the whole output.

Is this quiz useful if I don't have a business yet?

Yes. It works for personal brands, indie creators on X or LinkedIn, solopreneurs, and founders who haven't launched yet. The fundamentals, niche, voice, positioning, apply before you have revenue.

What do I do if I score low on niche?

A weak niche is the most common reason creators fail to get traction. Use the Ikigai framework to find where your passion, skill, market demand, and monetization potential overlap, IkigaiNiche is built exactly for this.
