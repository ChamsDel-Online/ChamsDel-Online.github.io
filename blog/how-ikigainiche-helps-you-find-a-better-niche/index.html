How IkigaiNiche helps you find a profitable business niche.

By Chaminda DelpagodageMarch 12, 2026Updated November 19, 2026Product story

The IkigaiNiche site describes the product as a free guided way to find a starting point when you have never had a business idea. It starts with you, not a clever idea: what you love, someone you would be glad to help, and a problem of theirs worth solving.

That is strong positioning because it avoids vague "find your passion" language. The live product is a free guided flow that takes about 10 minutes, with no sign-up. It asks a few simple questions, one at a time, and you can optionally email yourself a longer report with a private link.

What makes the workflow different

Instead of dumping users into endless idea generation, IkigaiNiche asks what you love, what you are good at, and who you would be glad to help. It then suggests people and problems that might fit, and gives an honest read on whether people already spend money on that problem. You also get a few ways you could start, a one-sentence starting point, your four ikigai circles, and three small first steps you can copy.

Why the right people find it

The live page speaks directly to what people actually search for: profitable niche, Ikigai method, niche finder, and business niche discovery. Those are specific enough to reach builders with a real need, not just people browsing generic founder advice.

Why it resonates with founders

The site also explains that the tool was built by someone trying to find a business that actually fits. That personal framing makes the product feel practical rather than abstract, which is exactly the right tone for builders trying to choose what to work on.
