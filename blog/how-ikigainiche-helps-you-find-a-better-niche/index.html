How IkigaiNiche helps you find a profitable business niche.

By Chaminda DelpagodageMarch 12, 2026Product story

The IkigaiNiche site describes the product as an interactive AI-powered tool for finding your profitable niche using the Ikigai method. More specifically, it walks users through discovering who they serve, what problem they solve, and why people will pay.

That is strong positioning because it avoids vague “find your passion” language. The product uses a structured process: skills and brainstorming, evaluation with a traffic-light system, and decision-making that ranks the top three niches as Gold, Silver, and Bronze.

What makes the workflow different

Instead of dumping users into endless idea generation, IkigaiNiche narrows the search with the Person + Problem formula. It helps users list their craft skills, pair those skills with specific people or companies, and score each combination across whether they like it, can help, and whether people will pay.

Why the right people find it

The live page speaks directly to what people actually search for: profitable niche, Ikigai method, niche finder, and business niche discovery. Those are specific enough to reach builders with a real need, not just people browsing generic founder advice.

Why it resonates with founders

The site also explains that the tool was built by someone trying to find a business that actually fits. That personal framing makes the product feel practical rather than abstract, which is exactly the right tone for builders trying to choose what to work on.
