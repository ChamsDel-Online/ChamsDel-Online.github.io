Why I built CompliQuiz.

By Chaminda DelpagodageMarch 12, 2026Product story

CompliQuiz is built around a question many small businesses struggle with early: which compliance frameworks actually apply to us? That question sounds simple, but it usually shows up after teams have already lost time reading the wrong standards or guessing at obligations they may not even have.

The clearest metadata on the current site says exactly what the tool is for: helping businesses find out which compliance frameworks apply to them. That positioning is much stronger than generic compliance content because it speaks to the first real pain point in the workflow.

The problem behind the product

For many businesses, compliance does not start with implementation. It starts with uncertainty. Teams need clarity on whether they should care about HIPAA, SOC 2, PCI, privacy frameworks, or something else entirely before they can plan responsibly.

What CompliQuiz is trying to do

CompliQuiz is meant to act as that first layer of clarity. Instead of dropping users into a maze of acronyms, it helps them identify which frameworks may matter to their business and where they should focus first.

Why this is a valuable SEO angle

People search for compliance help when they are confused, not when they already know the answer. That makes framework-identification a strong search intent to build around, and it gives CompliQuiz a more specific, more useful position than broad compliance advice.
