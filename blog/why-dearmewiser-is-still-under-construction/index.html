Why DearMeWiser is still under construction.

By Chaminda DelpagodageMarch 12, 2026Build note

DearMeWiser is being shaped as a reflective product for personal growth, clarity, and better conversations with yourself. That positioning sets a higher bar than a typical utility app, because the product has to feel trustworthy before it feels useful.

Unlike the other projects, there is not yet a fully readable public site with rich metadata to borrow from. What is clear from the current product positioning is that DearMeWiser is meant to support reflection, emotional clarity, and a more grounded inner dialogue.

Why that makes the build slower

Products in this category are easy to make shallow. It is easy to launch something full of prompts, advice, and generic self-help language. It is much harder to build something calm, credible, and worth returning to.

Why it is still under construction

I would rather take more time and shape the tone carefully than ship a rushed experience that feels loud or disposable. DearMeWiser needs to earn trust, and that makes patience part of the product strategy.
