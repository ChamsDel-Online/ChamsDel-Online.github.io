Questions

Common questions.

What is a fractional AI advisor?

A fractional AI advisor is a senior outside advisor who works with your company part time instead of being a full-time hire. I help regulated companies get AI approved by security and used by their teams, with a method called Govern, Enable, Measure.

How is this different from an AI consultant?

I do not sell an AI strategy deck or build your product AI. I work on workforce AI rollout: the policy, the approved tools, the training and the measurement that get AI approved and actually used. Your product AI and engineering roadmap stay with your CTO.

What does the AI Readiness Review cost?

From $3,750. It is two weeks with a fixed scope. The fee is credited toward the 90-Day Pilot Rollout if you book within 60 days.

Do you cover PCI, HIPAA and SOC 2?

These are the frameworks usually in scope for the companies I work with: fintech, payments, healthcare-adjacent and insurance. The Review looks at how AI is used against them. It is advice, not an audit, a certification or legal advice.
